Privacy Policy
MyLife: Life Notebook · Good Things Studio · Last updated: 28 October 2026

This policy covers MyLife, the app made by Barış Ertufan and Good Things Studio (“we”).

In short

  • MyLife needs no account and has no ads, no tracking and no analytics.
  • Everything you write is saved on your phone first, then only in your own iCloud account.
  • Your data is never sent to any server of ours; we cannot see it.

What information, and where?

Your food notes, notes about your partner, people, friends and film and series lists are information you enter in the app. It is stored on your iPhone and, if iCloud is on, synced to your private iCloud database through Apple’s CloudKit service. Only your Apple Account can access that database. You can turn iCloud off in the app’s Settings.

What goes over the internet?

  • Film and series search (TMDB): The text you type into search and the IDs of the titles you add are sent to The Movie Database (themoviedb.org) to fetch posters and details. These requests never include your name or your notes. TMDB privacy policy.
  • IMDb rating (optional, OMDb): The IMDb IDs of titles you add may be sent to OMDb to get their IMDb rating.
  • Place search (Apple Maps): When you add a food note, the place name you type and, if you allow it, your approximate location are sent to Apple to suggest places. Apple privacy policy.
  • iCloud (Apple CloudKit): Backup of your data and sync between your devices.

Location

Location permission is asked for only to suggest restaurants nearby while you search for a place. Your location is never stored or sent to us. The app works without it.

Face ID

Unlocking is handled by iOS. The app never sees face data; it only learns whether the unlock succeeded.

IMDb import

The IMDb export file (CSV) you choose is read only on your phone.

Deleting and exporting your data

You can delete any entry in the app. Settings → “Export all my data” gives you everything as a JSON file. To remove the iCloud copy too: iPhone Settings → [your name] → iCloud → Manage Storage → MyLife → Delete Data.

Children

MyLife is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect personal data from anyone.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, this page will be updated. For questions, write to us at hellogoodthingsstudio@gmail.com.
