Questions

Will I lose my data if I change phones?

No. Sign in to the new iPhone with the same Apple Account and keep iCloud on; when you install MyLife, everything comes back from iCloud.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Everything is saved on your phone first. When you’re back online, your changes go to iCloud by themselves. The small label on the home screen shows whether any changes are waiting.

How do I bring in my IMDb ratings?

IMDb doesn’t let apps sign in, but it does give you your lists as files:

  • On imdb.com, open Your Ratings from your profile and choose Export from the ⋯ menu.
  • When the file is ready, download the CSV from the Exports page. Do the same on your Watchlist page.
  • In MyLife, go to Films & TV → the ↓ icon at the top right → choose the files. A title is never added twice.

A sample file to try: sample-imdb.csv

Why are some posters missing?

Posters and details come from the internet (TMDB). Offline, or for titles TMDB doesn’t have, the app shows a poster it draws itself; the real poster loads by itself once you’re online.

Can I make my own photo the app icon?

iOS doesn’t let apps turn their own icon into a photo. You can pick one of four ready-made icons in Settings. For a photo: in the Shortcuts app, make a new shortcut → “Open App” → MyLife, then Share → “Add to Home Screen” and choose your photo as the icon.

How do I back up or delete my data?

Settings → “Export all my data” gives you a JSON file. To delete the iCloud copy: iPhone Settings → [your name] → iCloud → Manage Storage → MyLife.
